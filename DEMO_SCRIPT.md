# Second Sight FC Demo Script



## Goal



Show that Second Sight FC is not a pure prediction tool. It is an explainable pre-match AI companion that helps fans understand historical patterns, uncertainty, and model limitations.



Target length: 2 to 3 minutes.



---



## Demo Flow With Timing Cues



### 0:00 to 0:20 - Opening



Hi, I'm Zac, and this is Second Sight FC.



Second Sight FC is an explainable AI companion for World Cup fans. Instead of simply predicting who might win, it helps fans understand what historical signals inform a matchup, where the model is uncertain, and what the AI does not know.



The project focuses on explainability, trust, and fan understanding before the match.



---



### 0:20 to 0:45 - Show the Main App



Here, the user can choose two national teams.



For this demo, I'll start with Brazil vs Argentina.



The app gives a pattern-based reading. It shows outcome probabilities, but the emphasis is not on treating the model like an oracle. The emphasis is on understanding the signals behind the reading.



---



### 0:45 to 1:10 - Explain the Confidence Label



The confidence label shows whether the model sees the matchup as a toss-up, moderate confidence, or higher confidence.



This matters because a responsible AI tool should not just give an answer. It should also tell users how strongly or weakly the model supports that answer.



A toss-up is especially important because it shows the tool is willing to communicate uncertainty instead of pretending every matchup has a clear answer.



---



### 1:10 to 1:35 - Explain the Reasoning Section



The "What historical signals shaped this reading" section turns the model's historical signals into plain English.



For newer fans, the app explains concepts like historical win rate, average goals scored, defensive record, and recent form in simpler language.



This makes the tool more accessible to fans who may be watching the World Cup but do not follow football statistics closely.



---



### 1:35 to 1:55 - Show the Signal Comparison



The team signal comparison table lets fans see the historical evidence side by side.



This supports transparency because the user can see what the model is drawing from instead of only seeing a final number.



---



### 1:55 to 2:15 - Show the Trust Check



The Trust Check is one of the most important parts of the app.



Second Sight FC explains that the model does not know live injuries, lineups, weather, red cards, substitutions, tactics, travel fatigue, or emotional pressure inside the stadium.



That keeps the AI grounded. It helps users understand both what the model can do and what it cannot do.



---



### 2:15 to 2:35 - Show Learning Sections



The "How to use Second Sight FC" section explains that the app should be used as a pre-match conversation guide.



The "Learn the signals" section defines the major signals in plain language.



Together, these make the project more educational and fan-centered.



---



### 2:35 to 2:50 - Mention IBM Bob



IBM Bob was used during the development process to review and refine the prototype. Bob helped identify improvements around challenge alignment, explainability language, trust messaging, README structure, and final submission readiness.



---



### 2:50 to 3:00 - Closing



Second Sight FC helps fans understand the match behind the number.



It uses AI to make historical football patterns more explainable, more transparent, and easier for everyday fans to discuss before kickoff.



The goal is not to replace analysts, coaches, referees, or fans. The goal is to make AI-supported football insight more understandable and trustworthy.
